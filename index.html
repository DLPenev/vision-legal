Short videos, written, produced and published on a schedule.

Vision is a personal automation system. It researches a topic, writes a script, narrates it, cuts it to stock footage, burns in captions, checks the result against measurable quality rules, and posts the finished video to its owner's own social media accounts. One person operates it, on one computer, for their own channels.

What it is

Not a service, not a platform, and not open to sign-ups. Vision is software its owner runs on their own Mac to keep a small number of niche channels publishing consistently.

Single operator

There are no user accounts and no customers. The only person who can use it is the owner, at the keyboard of the machine it runs on.

Owner's accounts only

It publishes to social media accounts the owner controls and has personally authorised. It never touches anyone else's account or content.

Runs locally

Everything happens on one computer. There is no server, no hosted component and no database in the cloud holding anyone's data.

Labelled as AI

Every video is produced with AI assistance and is disclosed as such on each platform, using the platform's own AI-content flag as well as a line in the caption.

Checked before posting

Each video must pass measurable checks for length, resolution, loudness, audio sync, caption coverage and content policy. Anything that fails is never published.

Modest volume

About one video per channel per day, posted at fixed times. It is built for consistency, not for bulk uploading.

The channels

Each channel is a separate subject with its own voice, visual style and posting schedule. Scripts are written to the brief below and nothing is reproduced from other creators.

Psychology & human behaviour

Cognitive biases, memory, body language and social dynamics, explained in about a minute.

Business & founders

One concrete problem a small business owner faces, one honest reframe, one practical step.

Life coaching & motivation

Discipline, habits, confidence and getting unstuck, without clichés or hype.

Platform integrations

Vision publishes through each platform's official API, using credentials the owner granted through that platform's own sign-in screen. Tokens are stored in the operating system's keychain on the owner's computer and nowhere else.

TikTok

Content Posting API, direct post from file. Login Kit is used once so the owner can authorise their own account. Posts carry TikTok's AI-generated content flag.

YouTube Shorts

YouTube Data API upload, with the altered or synthetic content disclosure set on every video.

Instagram & Facebook Reels

Graph API publishing to the owner's own professional account and page, with the AI disclosure in the caption.

Questions

Can I sign up or use Vision?

No. It is one person's private tooling, not a product. There is no registration, no hosted version and nothing to buy.

Does it collect data about anyone?

No. It reads the performance statistics of the owner's own videos so it can decide what to publish next. It does not collect, store or process information about any other person or account. See the privacy policy.

Is the content AI-generated?

Yes, and it says so. Scripts and narration are produced with AI assistance, footage is licensed stock, and every post carries the platform's AI-content label plus a disclosure in the caption.

Who is responsible for what gets posted?

The owner, who configures the channels, sets the rules the scripts must follow, and can stop or remove anything at any time. Automated checks reject videos that fail the quality or policy rules before they ever reach a platform.
