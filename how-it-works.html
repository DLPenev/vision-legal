How a video gets made

Eleven stages run in order on the owner's computer. Each one saves its work, so a crash or a restart resumes where it stopped rather than starting again. A run takes ten to twenty minutes end to end.

The pipeline

Nothing here scrapes or reuses another creator's work. Footage is licensed stock, the script is written for the channel's brief, and the narration is synthesised.

  1. TopicA subject is chosen for the channel from its own list, from what is currently of interest in the niche, and from how earlier videos performed. Subjects already covered are never repeated.
  2. ResearchFacts, examples and a point of view are gathered for that subject.
  3. ScriptA sixty to seventy-five second narration is written in the channel's voice: a hook, the substance, and one thing the viewer can act on.
  4. ScenesThe script is split into shots, each with a visual description and a search term for stock footage.
  5. Captions for the postThe title, description and hashtags are written to each platform's conventions.
  6. FootageVertical clips are retrieved from licensed stock libraries. Several candidates per shot are compared against the sentence they illustrate and the closest match is used. A ledger prevents the same clip appearing across videos.
  7. NarrationThe script is spoken by a synthetic voice chosen for the channel.
  8. SubtitlesThe narration is transcribed back to get the timing of every word, which becomes word-by-word captions positioned clear of each platform's interface.
  9. AssemblyClips are cropped to vertical, cross-faded, overlaid with the hook and captions, and the audio is mastered to broadcast loudness.
  10. Quality gateThe finished file must pass every check below. A failure sends it back for one rebuild; a second failure ends the run and nothing is published.
  11. PublishThe video is uploaded through each platform's official API, carrying the AI-generated disclosure. Platforms are independent, so one failing does not block the others.

What the quality gate checks

These are measurements, not opinions. Every one is taken from the finished file.

Length

Between forty and one hundred seconds, and the script within a set word count.

Picture

1080 by 1920 at thirty frames per second, with no long stretch of black frames.

Sound

Mastered to the loudness platforms expect, with audio and video the same length to within a fraction of a second.

Captions

Present for the whole narration and finishing before the video does.

Content policy

The narration is reviewed against the advertiser and community rules of the platforms it will be posted to.

Evidence kept

Every check and its measured value is recorded with the run, so a rejection can always be explained.

Credentials and access

Vision never sees a password. For each platform the owner signs in on that platform's own page and grants access, and the resulting token is stored in the operating system keychain on the owner's computer. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the platform's settings, which immediately stops Vision from posting to that account.

The permissions requested are the minimum needed: the ability to upload a video to the owner's own account, and read access to the statistics of the videos it posted.
