Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026 · last updated 12 October 2026

Vision is a personal, single-user publishing tool operated by its owner on the owner's own computer. It publishes videos that the owner creates to social media accounts that the owner controls. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its owner.

Data the tool handles

  • Account authorisation tokens issued by TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook when the owner signs in. They are stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer and used only to publish the owner's videos and to read the performance statistics of those videos.
  • Basic profile information of the owner's own connected accounts, such as display name, avatar and account identifier, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Video performance statistics such as views, likes and follower counts for the owner's own accounts, stored locally to decide what to publish next.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process any data about other people or other accounts.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use cookies, tracking or advertising. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
  • No data leaves the owner's computer except the API requests needed to publish a video or read its statistics on the respective platform.

Where data is stored

Authorisation tokens live in the macOS Keychain. Video files, scripts and statistics live on the local disk of the owner's computer. There is no server, no hosted database and no backup held by any third party on the tool's behalf.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and statistics stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them or revokes the tool's access in the platform's settings, which immediately disables it for that account. Deleting the application's data folder removes everything it holds.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not usable by anyone other than its owner.
